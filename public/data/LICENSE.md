# Data licence

Files in this folder are derived data.

- `verbs.*`, `adjectives.*`, `nouns.*`, `motion.json`: derived from the OpenRussian.org dictionary export
  (https://github.com/Badestrand/russian-dictionary), itself built on Wiktionary. Licensed CC BY-SA 4.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). As share-alike material, these derived files are also CC BY-SA 4.0.
- `participles.*`, `diminutives.*`, `numerals.json`, and some comparatives, superlatives and noun genders in the files
  above: from Wiktionary (https://en.wiktionary.org) via kaikki.org's wiktextract extract. Wiktionary content is
  CC BY-SA 4.0 (and GFDL); these derived files are CC BY-SA 4.0.
- `learn.json`: the sentences Learn pages quote (Tatoeba, CC BY 2.0 FR, keyed by Tatoeba id) and pronunciations (IPA)
  from Wiktionary (CC BY-SA 4.0).
- `reading.json` and `reading.*.json`: texts by Leo Tolstoy (1828–1910) and Anton Chekhov (1860–1904), public domain,
  from Russian Wikisource at the revisions linked in the file (`works[].source`). The word meanings in them come from
  the OpenRussian and Wiktionary data above (CC BY-SA 4.0); meanings marked as ours and the questions are ours.
- `words.*`: words and meanings from the OpenRussian data above (CC BY-SA 4.0); the "other words" are ordered by their
  counts in FrequencyWords (https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords, OpenSubtitles 2018, CC BY-SA 4.0). These
  derived files are CC BY-SA 4.0.
- `sentences.*` and `cloze.*`: sentences and translations from Tatoeba (https://tatoeba.org), CC BY 2.0 FR
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/). Each row starts with the Tatoeba sentence id.

Generated by `npm run data` in this repository; see `reports/validation.md` for what was dropped and why.
