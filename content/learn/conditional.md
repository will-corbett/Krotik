The conditional (условное наклонение) expresses *would*, *would have* and unreal *if*. It's formed with the **past tense** plus the particle **бы**, whatever the time meant.

## Forming it

- past tense of the verb + бы: я бы сказал, она бы сказала, мы бы сказали;
- the past form agrees with the subject in gender and number, as usual;
- бы usually comes after the verb or after the first word of the clause.

## Unreal conditions: если бы

In an unreal *if*, both halves take бы, and the если half usually puts бы straight after если: *If I had time, I would go.* There is no separate past conditional: the same form covers *would* and *would have*, and context tells you which.
