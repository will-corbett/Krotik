Russian numbers decline. In speech people often simplify, but in writing every part of a number changes for case, and the noun after it changes too.

## один, два

**один** agrees with its noun like an adjective, and even has a plural (одни часы):

## The noun after a number

- In the **nominative** (and the accusative of things), 2–4 take the **genitive singular** and 5 and up the **genitive plural**: две книги, пять книг.
- In **every other case**, the number and the noun both take that case, with the noun in the plural: двум книгам, пятью книгами.
- In the **accusative of people and animals**, 2–4 look like the genitive: вижу двух друзей.

## Compound numbers

In a compound number every part declines: двадцать пять → двадцати пяти, двадцатью пятью.

## Collective numbers

двое, трое, четверо… count people, especially with nouns that only have a plural, and with pronouns: нас было трое.

::table numeral двое
