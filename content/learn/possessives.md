## его, её, их never change

The words for *his, her, its, their* (его, её, их) are frozen genitive forms of the personal pronouns. They never change for case, gender or number: его дом, его книга, в его доме.

## свой: one's own

**свой** refers back to the **subject** of its clause, whichever person that is: я взял свою книгу, ты взял свою книгу, он взял свою книгу.

In the third person this makes a real difference:

- он взял свою книгу: he took *his own* book;
- он взял его книгу: he took *his* (someone else's) book.

> In the first and second person, мой and твой are also fine where свой is possible. In the third person, swapping свой and его changes the meaning.
