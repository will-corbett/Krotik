In impersonal sentences (безличные предложения) there's no grammatical subject. The person who feels or needs something goes into the **dative**, and the main word is a fixed form like надо or холодно.

## Typical words

| Meaning | Words |
|---|---|
| need, must | надо, нужно |
| may, can | можно |
| mustn't, can't | нельзя |
| it's time | пора |
| feelings and states | холодно, жарко, скучно, весело, грустно, стыдно, страшно, плохо, хорошо |
| ease, difficulty | легко, трудно |

## The pattern

**dative person + word (+ infinitive)**: *I need to go*, *are you cold?*

In the past and future, add было or будет: *I was bored*, *you'll be cold*. These never change for the person.

> English *I am cold* has no Russian equivalent with я. Saying я холодный describes your character (*I'm a cold person*), not how you feel.
