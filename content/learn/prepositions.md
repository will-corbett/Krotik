Every preposition is followed by a particular case. Learn each preposition together with its case, and remember that a few can take two cases with different meanings.

## Prepositions by case

| Case | Prepositions |
|---|---|
| Genitive | у, без, для, до, из, от, около, после, кроме, против, с ("from, off") |
| Dative | к, по |
| Accusative | через, про, в and на (direction), за and под (direction) |
| Instrumental | с ("with"), над, перед, между, за and под (location) |
| Prepositional | о, при, в and на (location) |

## Two cases, two meanings

The most important pairs answer two different questions: **где?** ("where?", location) or **куда?** ("where to?", direction).

| | Location (где?) | Direction (куда?) |
|---|---|---|
| в, на | + prepositional | + accusative |
| за, под | + instrumental | + accusative |

**с** also has two meanings: "with" (+ instrumental) and "from, off" (+ genitive).

## Common mistakes

| Not this | But this | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Я иду в работу. | Я иду на работу. | **Wrong.** работа takes на, not в: на работу, на работе. So do почта, концерт and урок. |
| Я иду в школе. | Я иду в школу. | **Wrong.** With movement (куда?) в takes the accusative; the prepositional is for where something is (где?). |
| Я приехал из работы. | Я приехал с работы. | **Wrong.** A noun that takes на takes с for “from”: на работу → с работы. |
