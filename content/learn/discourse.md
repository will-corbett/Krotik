Linking words show how one sentence follows from the last: a contrast, something added, an order, a conclusion, or an
aside. They are what makes Russian sound joined up rather than a list of sentences. Most stand at the start of a
sentence and are followed by a comma.

## Contrast: однако, тем не менее, впрочем, зато

- **однако**: *however*. Like но, but it can start a sentence, and it's more formal.
- **тем не менее**: *nevertheless*, *even so*.
- **впрочем**: *mind you*, *though*: it adds a reservation to what you've just said.
- **зато**: *but then*, *on the other hand*: something good that makes up for something bad.

## Adding: кроме того, к тому же

Both mean *besides*, *what's more*. **К тому же** is a little more conversational.

## Drawing a conclusion: итак, таким образом, в общем

- **итак**: *so*, *well then*: picking up the thread or moving on.
- **таким образом**: *thus*, *in this way*. Formal: essays, reports.
- **в общем**: *anyway*, *in short*. Spoken.

## An aside: кстати

**Кстати** is *by the way*: it brings in something that has just occurred to you.

## Commas

These words are set off by commas, before and after: Во-первых, это слишком дорого. Однако is the one exception to
watch: when it simply means *but*, joining two clauses (Он устал, однако работал), there's no comma after it.

## Common mistakes

| Not this | But this | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Кроме того я не умею танцевать. | Кроме того, я не умею танцевать. | Wrong: a linking word at the start is followed by a comma. |
| Я устал, зато я не пойду. | Я устал, поэтому я не пойду. | Wrong: зато brings something good to balance the bad; *so* is поэтому. |
| В общем, данные показывают рост. (in a report) | Таким образом, данные показывают рост. | Correct, but в общем is spoken; a report needs таким образом. |
| Кроме, у меня есть дочь. | Кроме того, у меня есть дочь. | Wrong: кроме alone means *except* and needs a noun after it. |
