Many adjectives also have a **short form** (краткая форма). It is used only after the noun, as the predicate (*the book is interesting*), never before the noun. It has four forms (masculine, feminine, neuter and plural) and doesn't change for case.

## Forming the short form

Take the long form, remove its ending, and add:

| Masculine | Feminine | Neuter | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| nothing | -а | -о | -ы / -и |

When the stem ends in two consonants, the masculine often gains a vowel: о or е between them.

::table adjective новый

The short forms are in the bottom rows of the table. Watch the **stress**: in many short forms it moves to the ending in the feminine (нова́), and sometimes in the plural too.

::table adjective хороший

## When to use it

- **Temporary states or a specific situation**: он болен ("he is ill right now");
- **with a complement**: он готов к экзамену; она похожа на мать;
- **some adjectives are almost always short**: рад, должен, нужен, похож.

The long form describes a permanent quality, or is used before the noun: больной человек ("a sick man"), новая книга.
