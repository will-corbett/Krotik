Russian stress (ударение) is free: it can fall on any syllable, and it often **moves** within a word as the ending changes. Dictionaries and learner books mark it with an acute accent, as this site does; ordinary Russian text doesn't.

## Why it matters

- **Unstressed vowels are reduced**: an unstressed о sounds like а, and an unstressed е or я like и. Stress changes how the whole word sounds.
- **Some words differ only by stress**: замок ("castle") and замок ("lock") are spelled the same.
- **ё is always stressed**, so words with ё need no mark.

## Stress moves

In many verbs, stress is on the ending in the я form and the infinitive, then moves back for every other person:

> There are patterns, but no simple rules: the reliable way is to learn each word with its stress, and to notice where it moves.
