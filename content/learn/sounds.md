Russian spelling shows the sounds fairly well, but not exactly. A handful of regular rules change how letters are said; once you know them, you can read most words aloud correctly. Each word below is shown with its pronunciation in the International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA), taken from Wiktionary.

## Reading the IPA

You don't need to learn IPA to use this page: compare each word with its transcription and look for the change described. A few symbols help:

| Symbol | Means |
|---|---|
| ˈ | the next syllable is stressed |
| ʲ | the consonant before it is soft |
| ɐ, ə | a weak "uh": unstressed о and а |
| ɪ | a short "i": unstressed е and я |
| ɨ | the sound of ы |
| ʂ, ʐ | ш and ж (always hard) |
| t͡ɕ, ɕː | ч and щ (always soft) |
| t͡s | ц |
| x | х |
| ɫ | a hard ("dark") л |

Sounds in brackets in a transcription are optional: some speakers say them, others don't.

## Unstressed vowels are reduced

Only the stressed vowel is said in full. Unstressed **о** sounds like **а** just before the stress, and like a weak "uh" elsewhere. Unstressed **е** and **я** sound like a short **и**.

::ipa молоко хорошо окно сейчас сестра теперь часы

This is why stress matters so much: it decides how every vowel in the word sounds. молоко has three о, but only the last one is a real о.

## Voiced consonants lose their voice at the end of a word

At the end of a word, б, в, г, д, ж, з are said like п, ф, к, т, ш, с:

::ipa хлеб друг город нож глаз сад

## Neighbouring consonants match

When two consonants meet, the first takes on the voicing of the second: voiceless before a voiceless consonant, voiced before a voiced one. (в, л, м, н, р and й don't change the consonant before them.)

::ipa водка ложка просьба сделать вокзал футбол всё

## Hard and soft consonants

Most consonants come in pairs, hard and soft. A soft consonant is said with the middle of the tongue raised towards the roof of the mouth, as if a short "y" were mixed in. The letter after it shows which: е, ё, и, ю, я and ь make it soft. Softness can change the meaning:

::ipa мат мать был бил лук люк брат брать

Three consonants are always hard, whatever letter follows: ж, ш and ц. So жизнь and цирк are said with ы, not и. Two are always soft: ч and щ.

::ipa жизнь шесть цирк час щи

## Silent consonants

In some groups of consonants, one is written but not said:

::ipa солнце здравствуйте чувство сердце праздник лестница

## Spellings to learn by heart

A few common spellings don't match the sound at all:

- **г** sounds like **в** in the endings -ого and -его, and in его and сегодня.
- **ч** sounds like **ш** in что (and чтобы), and in конечно.
- The endings **-тся** and **-ться** both sound like **-ца**, so учится and учиться sound almost the same.
- с + ч and ж + ч sound like **щ**: счастье, мужчина.
- **г** before к sounds like **х**: лёгкий, мягкий.

::ipa его сегодня нового что конечно учится учиться счастье мужчина лёгкий мягкий

## Common mistakes

| Not this | But this | Why |
|---|---|---|
| молоко read letter by letter: *mo-lo-ko* | *ma-la-KO*, with only the last о in full | Wrong: unstressed о is reduced. |
| хлеб with a final *b* | *khlep* | Wrong (it sounds foreign): a final consonant loses its voice. |
| что with *ch* | *shto* | Wrong: ч is said as ш in что. |
| здравствуйте with every letter said | *ZDRAST-vuy-tye* | Unnatural: the first в is silent. |
| мат for "mother" | мать, with a soft т | Another word: мат is "checkmate", мать is "mother". |
