After a single-word comparative (старше, лучше, быстрее), the thing compared can be expressed in two ways, both meaning "than".

## The genitive, or чем

- **genitive**, with no word for "than": он старше брата;
- **чем**, after a comma, with the noun in its normal case: он старше, чем брат.

Both mean *he is older than his brother*.

## When you must use чем

- with более / менее + adjective: более интересный, чем…;
- when what you compare isn't in the nominative: *it's warmer in the house than in the street*;
- with the possessives его, её, их, which can't take the genitive form.
