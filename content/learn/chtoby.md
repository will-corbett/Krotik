**чтобы** introduces a purpose ("in order to") or what someone wants or asks for. What follows it depends on **who** does the action.

## Same subject: the infinitive

When the person in both parts is the same, чтобы is followed by the **infinitive**: *I eat (in order) to live*.

::examples chtoby inf 3

## Different subject: the past tense

When someone else is meant to do it, чтобы is followed by the **past tense**, even if the meaning is present or future. This is how Russian expresses wishes and requests: *tell him to leave*, *I want you to come*.

::examples chtoby past 3

> Verbs of wanting, asking and ordering (хотеть, просить, сказать in the sense of "tell to") take чтобы + past when the action is someone else's.
