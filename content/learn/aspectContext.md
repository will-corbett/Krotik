Knowing a verb's partner is only half the job: in a real sentence you have to choose. The aspect page explains what each aspect means; this page is about the clues in the sentence that point one way or the other.

## Clues for the imperfective

- **repetition or habit**: часто, всегда, обычно, каждый день;
- **duration**: долго, весь день, целый час;
- **the process, not the result**: *what were you doing?*;
- **after verbs of beginning, continuing and finishing**: начать, продолжать, кончить are always followed by an imperfective infinitive;
- **the general fact**: *have you ever read it?*

## Clues for the perfective

- **one complete action with a result**: уже, наконец;
- **a sudden single event**: вдруг, сразу;
- **one step in a sequence**: *he came in, sat down and opened the book*;
- **ability or success on one occasion**: смог, успел.

> When both seem possible, ask: does the result of one occasion matter (perfective), or the action itself, its repetition or its duration (imperfective)?
