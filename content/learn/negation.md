Russian negation (отрицание) has three features that English doesn't: the genitive after нет, double negatives, and the little word ни.

## нет + genitive

To say something isn't there, or someone doesn't have something, use **нет** + **genitive**. In the past and future use не было and не будет, still with the genitive.

::examples negation net 3

## Double negatives are required

Negative pronouns and adverbs (никто, ничто, никогда, нигде…) always come with **не** before the verb: *I don't know anybody* is literally "I nobody not know". никто and ничто decline like кто and что.

::examples negation nipron 3

## не or ни?

- **не** is the ordinary "not";
- **ни** means "not a single", appears in ни… ни… ("neither… nor"), and in fixed phrases like ни разу.

::examples negation ne-ni 2
