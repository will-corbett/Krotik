Conjunctions (союзы) join a clause to the rest of the sentence. Four are especially easy to mix up, and the clause after each always follows a comma.

## что, чтобы, если, когда

- **что** ("that") reports a fact, something said, known or felt.
- **чтобы** ("so that", "for someone to") gives a purpose, or what someone wants or asks for. After чтобы the verb is in the infinitive or past tense.
- **если** ("if") states a condition.
- **когда** ("when") gives a time.

## If or whether?

English *if* sometimes means *whether*: *I don't know if he'll come*. Russian uses **ли** for that, never если.
