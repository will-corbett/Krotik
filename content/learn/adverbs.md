A verbal adverb (деепричастие) turns a verb into a phrase meaning "while doing" or "having done". It never changes its ending, and its subject is always the same as the subject of the main verb.

It isn't the same as an ordinary adverb (наречие) like быстро or хорошо: those come from adjectives and have their own topic, Adverbs.

## Two kinds

| | Imperfective | Perfective |
|---|---|---|
| Meaning | *while doing*: at the same time | *having done*: before the main action |
| Formed from | the они form, minus its ending, plus **-я** (or **-а** after ж, ш, щ, ч) | the past stem plus **-в** (**-вшись** for -ся verbs) |
| Example | читая | прочитав |

> Verbal adverbs belong to written Russian and careful speech. In conversation the same idea is usually expressed with когда or a second verb.

## Some irregular ones

Wiktionary lists the forms used in practice, and a few don't follow the pattern: идти gives идя, and быть gives будучи. The drills always use the attested forms.
