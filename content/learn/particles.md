Particles (частицы) are short words that add emphasis, doubt or a tone of voice. They're hard to translate one-to-one, so it helps to see them in real sentences.

## ли: questions and "whether"

**ли** turns a statement into a yes/no question, and introduces an indirect question: "whether". It comes straight after the word being asked about, usually the verb.

::examples particles li 2

## же and ведь

- **же** adds emphasis ("so, then, after all") and also means "the same" after тот: тот же.
- **ведь** appeals to something both people know: "after all, you know".

## вот, ну, даже, только

- **вот**: "here is, there", pointing something out;
- **ну**: "well…", hesitating or urging;
- **даже**: "even";
- **только**: "only, just".
