Reported speech (косвенная речь) tells what someone said, asked or wanted, without quoting their words. Russian uses the same words as other clauses: что, ли, a question word, or чтобы. The big difference from English: **the tense doesn't change**.

## Statements: что, and keep the tense

Keep the tense the speaker used. English moves it back (*is* becomes *was*, *will* becomes *would*); Russian doesn't.

::tatoeba 3769451:беден 4611213:придёт 7192522:был

- In the first, Tom's own words were in the present (я беден): *Tom said he was poor*.
- In the second, the future stays future: *he would come*.
- Use the past only if the speaker did: in the third, Tom said he had been ill.

The pronouns change as they do in English: the speaker's я becomes он or она.

## Yes/no questions: ли

A reported yes/no question uses **ли**, never если. The word being asked about comes first, then ли, then the rest. The question «Ты любишь китайскую кухню?» becomes:

## Asking someone to do something: the infinitive

After **просить** (*ask*), **велеть** (*tell, order*) and **советовать** (*advise*), the person is the object and the
action is an infinitive. It's shorter than чтобы, and means the same when the person asked is the one who'll do it:

::tatoeba 3673998:помочь 5582079:уйти

## In conversation: мол

In speech, **мол** marks someone else's words without quoting them exactly, often with a hint of doubt, like *he said
he was, like, …*:

::tatoeba 9946842:мол 4732590:мол

## Verbs that report speech

- сказать / говорить: *say*, *tell*.
- спросить / спрашивать: *ask* a question.
- попросить / просить: *ask for* something, or ask someone to do something.
- ответить / отвечать: *answer*.
- объяснить / объяснять: *explain*.

## Common mistakes

| Not this | But this | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Он сказал, что он был беден. (reporting «я беден») | Он сказал, что он беден. | Correct, with another meaning: был says he had been poor before. |
| Она спросила, если я знаю её адрес. | Она спросила, знаю ли я её адрес. | Wrong: *whether* is ли. |
| Он попросил, где я живу. | Он спросил, где я живу. | Wrong: попросить is to ask *for* something; a question needs спросить. |
| Том сказал мне, чтобы я подожду. | Том сказал мне, чтобы я подождал. | Wrong: after чтобы with a new subject, the verb goes in the past. |
| Он попросил меня, чтобы помочь. | Он попросил меня помочь. | Wrong: when the person asked will do it, use the infinitive. |
