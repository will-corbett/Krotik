Ordinal numbers (порядковые числительные) say *which one*: first, second, third. They are adjectives in form, so they agree with their noun in gender, number and case.

## Compound ordinals

In a compound ordinal only the **last** word is an ordinal and changes; the others stay cardinal numbers: двадцать первый, на двадцать пятом этаже.

## Dates

A date uses the **neuter** ordinal (the word число is understood), then the month in the **genitive**:

- Сегодня пятое мая. (*Today is the fifth of May.*)
- Я приеду пятого мая. (*I'll come on the fifth of May*: on a date, the ordinal is genitive too.)

## Centuries and years

Centuries are masculine (век), and *in* a century is в + prepositional: двадцатый век, в двадцатом веке. They are usually written in Roman numerals: в XX веке.

A year is an ordinal too: в две тысячи двадцать шестом году (*in 2026*).

## Fractions

Fractions are a cardinal number plus the ordinal in the **genitive plural**, feminine (the word доля, *part*, is understood): две пятых, три четвёртых. For one: одна вторая.

Everyday fractions have their own nouns: половина, треть, четверть. The noun after any fraction is in the genitive: половина книги, треть пути.

полтора (*one and a half*), полторы with feminine nouns, takes the genitive singular: полтора часа, полторы минуты.
