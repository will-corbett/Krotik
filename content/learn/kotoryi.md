**который** ("who, which, that") joins a relative clause to a noun. It declines like an adjective, and its form comes from two places at once.

## Two questions to ask

1. **Gender and number** come from the noun it refers to (the one before the comma).
2. **Case** comes from its role in its *own* clause: subject, object, after a preposition, and so on.

So in *the book that I'm reading*, который is feminine (книга) but accusative, because it's the object of *reading*: которую.

> A comma always comes before который. A preposition comes before it too, never at the end of the clause as in English: *the house I live in* is дом, в котором я живу.
