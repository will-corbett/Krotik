Russian is written in the Cyrillic alphabet: 33 letters. It looks strange at first, but it takes only a few days to learn, and most letters always stand for the same sound.

## Five letters you already know

А, К, М, О and Т look and sound much as they do in English: мама, кот, там.

## False friends

These look like Latin letters but stand for different sounds. They cause most early mistakes:

| Letter | Sounds like | Not |
|---|---|---|
| В в | v | b |
| Е е | ye (as in *yes*) | e |
| Н н | n | h |
| Р р | r (rolled) | p |
| С с | s | c |
| У у | oo (as in *boot*) | y |
| Х х | kh (as in Scottish *loch*) | x |

So ресторан is *restoran*, and сок is *sok*.

## Two signs with no sound

- **ь** (soft sign) makes the consonant before it soft: соль, мать.
- **ъ** (hard sign) is rare; it keeps the next vowel separate: объект.

## Stress and the letter о

Each word has one stressed syllable. Unstressed **о** sounds like **а**: молоко sounds like *malako*. Krotik shows the stress with an accent mark (молоко́); ordinary Russian text doesn't, so learn each word with its stress.
