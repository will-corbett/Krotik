The basic rule (the imperfective for a process or a habit, the perfective for one result) covers most sentences. A few
uses go further, and they are where learners most often choose the wrong verb.

## Have you ever…? The fact itself

To ask or say whether something happened at all, Russian uses the **imperfective**, even for one action that was
finished. The question is about the fact, not the result.

::tatoeba 4551003:читал 4763308:видел

Answering «Читал» means *yes, I have (read it)*. Прочитал would put the weight on finishing it: *I've finished it*.

## Came and went

The imperfective past of a motion verb often means the action was reversed: someone came and has gone again. The
perfective says the result is still there.

::tatoeba 8675635:приходил 3253567:приходил

So Ко мне приходила сестра is *my sister came to see me* (and left), while Ко мне пришла сестра is *my sister has come*
(she's here now).

## Trying and succeeding

With some verbs the imperfective past means you tried, and the perfective that you succeeded: сдавал экзамен (*took*
an exam), сдал (*passed* it); уговаривал (*tried to persuade*), уговорил (*talked into it*).

::tatoeba 2229790:сдавала 3057530:уговорил

## Don't: the imperfective

After не, an imperative is usually **imperfective**: Не звони ей. A **perfective** after не is a warning: *mind you
don't …*.

::tatoeba 2480466:звони 1892930:упади 11623672:забудь

## Please, come in: the imperfective to invite

An imperfective imperative is the polite way to invite someone to do what they were going to do anyway: Садитесь!
Проходите! A perfective imperative asks for one particular action: Сядьте сюда.

::tatoeba 381258:Садитесь

## Common mistakes

| Not this | But this | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Ты когда-нибудь прочитал эту книгу? | Ты когда-нибудь читал эту книгу? | Unnatural: asking whether it ever happened takes the imperfective. |
| Ко мне пришла сестра. (she visited and left) | Ко мне приходила сестра. | Correct, with another meaning: пришла says she's still here. |
| Я сдавал экзамен. (meaning you passed) | Я сдал экзамен. | Correct, with another meaning: сдавал only says you took it. |
| Не забывай ключи! (meaning *this time*) | Не забудь ключи! | Correct, with another meaning: не забывай is a general rule. |
