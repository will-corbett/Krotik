A handful of Soviet cartoons and films are known by almost everyone, across generations and across the countries where Russian is spoken. Their lines turn up in everyday conversation.

## Cartoons

| Cartoon | What it is |
|---|---|
| Ну, погоди! | a wolf forever chasing a hare, almost without words |
| Винни-Пух | the Soviet Winnie-the-Pooh, with its own songs and jokes |
| Чебурашка | a furry creature of unknown species and his friend, the crocodile Гена |
| Ёжик в тумане | a short, dreamlike film about a hedgehog lost in fog |

Most of these are on the studio's official Soyuzmultfilm channel (see the links on the Culture page). They are short, and many are simple enough for beginners.

## Films

New Year's Eve on television traditionally means a few comedies from the 1960s and 1970s, which people quote all year round. A good first film to try is an Odesa or Gorky studio comedy with subtitles; both studios put their films online for free on their own channels.

> Watch on the studios' own channels, not on re-uploads: it's free, legal and usually better quality.
