Uzbekistan is the most populous country in Central Asia, and Russian is still its main second language.

## Russian here

Uzbek is the only state language, and Russian has no official status. Even so, it remains a common language in Tashkent and other cities, in business and in higher education, and many signs are in both Uzbek and Russian.

## How many people speak it

Figures vary: estimates for 2004 put active speakers of Russian at about 5 million, while the World Factbook gives 14% of the population. More people understand Russian than speak it every day.

## Local words

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| плов | rice cooked with meat and carrots, the national dish |
| лагман | thick noodles in a meat and vegetable sauce |
| самса | a baked pastry filled with meat |
| чайхана | a teahouse |
| кишлак | a village |

## Good to know

- Food words travel with the dishes: плов, лагман and самса are as familiar in Moscow as in Tashkent.
