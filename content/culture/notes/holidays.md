The biggest holiday of the year is New Year, not Christmas, and most greetings follow one pattern: с + the instrumental.

## Greetings

Almost every congratulation is с + the holiday in the instrumental case (творительный падеж); the verb поздравляю (*I congratulate*) is understood:

- С Новым годом! (*Happy New Year!*)
- С днём рождения! (*Happy birthday!*)
- С Рождеством! (*Merry Christmas!*)
- С праздником! (*Happy holiday!*, for any holiday)

The full form is Поздравляю тебя (or вас) с днём рождения! Birthday wishes usually go on with a list of good things: Желаю счастья, здоровья, успехов! The things wished for are in the genitive, after желаю.

## The main holidays

| Date | Holiday | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 31 December – 1 January | Новый год | The big family celebration: a decorated tree (ёлка), presents, a long dinner, the countdown at midnight. |
| 7 January | Рождество | Orthodox Christmas, by the old calendar; quieter than New Year, and a religious holiday for believers. |
| late winter | Масленица | A week of pancakes (блины) before Lent, saying goodbye to winter. |
| 8 March | Международный женский день | Flowers and presents for women: mothers, colleagues, teachers. |
| 22 March | Nauryz | The spring new year in Kazakhstan and Central Asia: a public holiday with family visits and a special festive soup. |
| spring | Пасха | Orthodox Easter: painted eggs and a sweet Easter bread; the date changes every year. |

## New Year

New Year is when families gather. The table is laid for hours, with salads that are almost obligatory (the best known is a potato salad called Olivier), and at midnight everyone raises a glass. Children get presents from Дед Мороз, *Grandfather Frost*, and his granddaughter Снегурочка. The holidays then run on into the first week or so of January.

> Birthday presents are given on the day or after, never before: wishing someone happy birthday early is thought to bring bad luck.

## Toasts

A meal with guests has toasts, often long ones: За здоровье! (*To your health!*), За вас! (*To you!*), За дружбу! (*To friendship!*). The word is за + the accusative.

## At work and school

On a colleague's birthday, people often bring a cake or sweets to share. On 1 September, the first day of the school year, pupils bring flowers for their teacher.
