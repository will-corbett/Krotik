The biggest holiday of the year is New Year, not Christmas, and most greetings follow one pattern: с + the instrumental.

## Greetings

Almost every congratulation is с + the holiday in the instrumental case (творительный падеж); the verb поздравляю (*I congratulate*) is understood:

- С Новым годом! (*Happy New Year!*)
- С днём рождения! (*Happy birthday!*)
- С Рождеством! (*Merry Christmas!*)
- С праздником! (*Happy holiday!*, for any holiday)

## The main holidays

| Date | Holiday | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 31 December – 1 January | Новый год | The big family celebration: a decorated tree (ёлка), presents, a long dinner, the countdown at midnight. |
| 7 January | Рождество | Orthodox Christmas, by the old calendar; quieter than New Year. |
| late winter | Масленица | A week of pancakes (блины) before Lent. |
| 8 March | Международный женский день | Flowers and presents for women. |
| 22 March | Nauryz | The spring new year in Kazakhstan and Central Asia: a public holiday with family visits and a special festive soup. |

> Birthday presents are given on the day or after, never before: wishing someone happy birthday early is thought to bring bad luck.

## Toasts

A meal with guests has toasts: За здоровье! (*To your health!*), За вас! (*To you!*). The word is за + the accusative.
