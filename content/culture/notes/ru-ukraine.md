Ukraine has many native speakers of Russian, and many people who speak both Ukrainian and Russian.

## Russian here

Ukrainian is the only state language under the 1996 Constitution. Russian has been most common in the large cities of the east and south, while Ukrainian is used in public life, schools and the media. Many people switch between the two, and some speak суржик, a mixture of Ukrainian and Russian that varies from place to place.

## How Russian sounds here

- г is pronounced as a soft, breathy sound, as in Ukrainian, rather than a hard [g].
- Some everyday words come from Ukrainian or sound Ukrainian:

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| шо | what (for что) |
| хто | who (for кто) |
| мг | mhm, yes |
| хата | home, a house |
| батько | father, dad |

## Good to know

- These words are informal: in writing and formal speech, standard Russian что and кто are used.
