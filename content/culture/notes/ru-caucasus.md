In Georgia, Armenia and Azerbaijan, Russian is a foreign language, but one that many people speak.

## Russian here

None of the three countries gives Russian an official role, though Georgia and Armenia recognise it as a minority language. It is spoken more by older people and in the capitals, and it is often the shared language between people of different nationalities in the region. English is now the first foreign language for many young people.

## How many people speak it

The censuses give very different pictures: in Georgia (2019), 87% of those asked said they speak Russian; in Armenia (2022), 65.3%; in Azerbaijan (2019), 6.7%.

## Local words

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| джан | dear (an affectionate word after a name; Armenia) |
| батоно | sir (a polite form of address to a man; Georgia) |
| хачапури | cheese-filled bread |
| лаваш | thin flat bread |

## Good to know

- Forms of address carry over into Russian: a guest in Yerevan may be called by name plus джан, and in Tbilisi a man may be addressed as батоно.
