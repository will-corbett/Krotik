In Belarus, Russian is the language most people use most of the time.

## Russian here

Belarus has two state languages, Belarusian and Russian, and Russian dominates in government, the media, education and the cities. In the countryside many people speak трасянка, a mixture of Belarusian and Russian.

## How Russian sounds here

Belarusian Russian is easy to recognise by its г, often a soft, breathy sound as in Belarusian rather than a hard [g]. Belarusian words slip into everyday Russian.

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| бульба | potato (in Belarus) |
| трасянка | Belarusian-Russian mixed speech |

## Good to know

- Belarusian and Russian are close relatives: many words are the same or nearly so, which is why mixing them is so easy.
