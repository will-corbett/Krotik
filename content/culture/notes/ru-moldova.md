In Moldova, Russian is a common second language and the first language of a sizeable minority.

## Russian here

The state language is Romanian. Russian has the status of a minority language, but it is widely understood, especially in Chișinău and among older people, and it is an official language in the autonomous region of Gagauzia, where it is the main language of everyday life alongside Gagauz.

## How many people speak it

In the 2024 census, Russian was the mother tongue of 11.1% of the population; many more speak it as a second language.
