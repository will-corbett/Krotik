Latvia and Estonia have large Russian-speaking communities; in Lithuania the community is smaller.

## Russian here

In all three countries the national language (Latvian, Estonian, Lithuanian) is the only official language, and Russian is a minority or foreign language. Russian-speaking communities have their own media, theatres and comedy, especially in Riga, Tallinn, Daugavpils and Narva. Among younger people, English has largely taken the place of Russian as the common foreign language.

## How many people speak it

- **Latvia**: in 2017, Russian was the native language of about 36% of the population.
- **Estonia**: in 2021, about 887,000 people, 66.6% of those asked, said they speak Russian.
- **Lithuania**: in 2021, about 67.4% said they speak Russian, mostly as a foreign language learned at school.

## How Russian sounds here

Baltic Russian is close to the standard; you'll hear local place names and words for local things, and people often switch languages in mid-sentence.
