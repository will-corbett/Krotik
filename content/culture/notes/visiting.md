Being invited home (в гости) is a sign of friendship. A few customs are worth knowing before you go.

## Arriving

- Take your shoes off at the door; the host will usually offer slippers (тапочки).
- Bring something: a cake (торт), sweets, fruit, or flowers for the hostess. Arriving with empty hands feels wrong.
- Flowers are given in **odd** numbers: three, five, seven. Even numbers are for funerals.
- Arriving a little late is fine for a dinner at home; arriving early can catch the host unprepared.

> Don't shake hands or pass things across the threshold (порог): step inside first. It's a superstition, but many people still keep to it.

## Greeting

Men usually shake hands with men; friends may hug. Kissing on the cheek is common among women who know each other well, and between close friends and family. With people you don't know, a smile and Здравствуйте! is enough.

## At the table

The host will offer more food several times. It's polite to try everything, and fine to say Спасибо, я больше не могу (*thank you, I can't eat any more*). An empty plate is often read as *I'd like some more*, so leave a little if you've had enough.

Help is usually refused at first. Offer once or twice anyway: Вам помочь? (*Can I help you?*)

## Conversation

Meals at home are long and talkative. Questions that might seem personal elsewhere, about family, work or money, are often asked out of friendly interest. Compliments on the food are always welcome.

## Leaving

Goodbyes often take a while, and the host may walk you to the door, or to the bus stop. Typical words:

- Спасибо за гостеприимство! (*Thank you for having me!*)
- Спасибо за вкусный ужин!
- Приходите ещё! (*Come again!*, said by the host)

A message the next day to say thank you is a nice touch.
