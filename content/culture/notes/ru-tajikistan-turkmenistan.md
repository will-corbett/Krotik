Tajikistan and Turkmenistan are neighbours with very different places for Russian.

## Tajikistan

Tajik is the state language, and the Constitution names Russian the language of communication between peoples (язык межнационального общения). Laws are published in both languages, Russian is taught in every school from the second year, and there are Russian-language and mixed Tajik-Russian schools.

In 2010, about 2 million people, 25.9% of those asked, said they speak Russian.

## Turkmenistan

Turkmen is the only state language; Russian lost its official role in 1996, and fewer young people now learn it. It is still understood by many in Ashgabat and by older people.

In 2022, about 1.4 million people, 19.3% of those asked, said they speak Russian.
