Kyrgyzstan is one of only two Central Asian countries where Russian is an official language.

## Russian here

The state language is Kyrgyz; under the Constitution, Russian is also an official language. In Bishkek, Russian is heard everywhere and is often the language of business, universities and the internet; outside the capital, Kyrgyz is more common.

## How many people speak it

In the 2022 census, about 3.4 million people, 49.5% of those asked, said they speak Russian. Most of them speak it as a second language alongside Kyrgyz.

## Local words

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| юрта | a yurt, the felt tent of nomads |
| кумыс | fermented mare's milk |
| аксакал | a respected elder |
| джигит | a skilled horseman; a brave young man |

## Good to know

- Horse games and the yurt are part of everyday culture, not only of festivals: you'll meet these words in Russian conversation.
