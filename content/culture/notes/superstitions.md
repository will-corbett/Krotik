Few people say they believe in omens (приметы), but many still follow them, half as a joke. You'll see these ones.

## Before a journey

Before leaving on a trip, everyone sits down for a moment in silence (присесть на дорожку). Then they get up and go.

If you have to go back for something you forgot, look in a mirror before leaving again. Some people also avoid starting anything important on a Monday.

## At home

- Don't shake hands or pass things across a threshold (порог).
- Don't whistle indoors: you'll whistle your money away.
- Don't sit at the corner of a table: you won't get married for seven years.
- Don't celebrate a birthday early.
- Don't leave empty bottles on the table: put them on the floor.

## Gifts

- Knives are not given as presents; if you are given one, give a small coin back, so that you have *bought* it.
- An empty purse or wallet is never given: put a coin inside.
- Flowers come in odd numbers; even numbers are for funerals.

## Luck

- To avoid bad luck after saying something hopeful, knock on wood (постучать по дереву) and say тьфу-тьфу-тьфу, pretending to spit over your left shoulder.
- A black cat (чёрная кошка) crossing your path is bad luck.
- Spilling salt (соль) means a quarrel is coming.
- Before an exam, students are wished Ни пуха ни пера! The answer is К чёрту! Saying спасибо would bring bad luck.

## Good signs

- Sitting between two people with the same name means you can make a wish.
- If your nose itches, you'll have a drink soon; if your ears burn, someone is talking about you.

> Most people keep these customs out of habit, not belief. Following them as a guest is a friendly gesture, and nobody expects you to know them all.
