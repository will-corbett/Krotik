Kazakhstan is the country outside Russia where Russian is most widely spoken.

## Russian here

The state language is Kazakh (казахский язык). Under the Constitution (Article 7), Russian is used officially on an equal footing with Kazakh in state bodies and local government, so forms, signs and offices are usually in both. Russian is the main language of business and of many families in the cities, especially Almaty, Astana and the north; Kazakh is heard more in the south and west and in the countryside. Many people move between the two, sometimes in one sentence.

## How many people speak it

In the 2021 census, about 14.4 million people, 83.7% of those asked, said they speak Russian.

## Local words

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| аким | the head of a city or region |
| акимат | the city or regional administration |
| той | a big celebration, such as a wedding |
| бешбармак | the national dish of meat and noodles |
| кумыс | fermented mare's milk |
| аксакал | a respected elder (literally "white beard") |

## Good to know

- Kazakh names are used with Russian grammar where they fit: a male surname in -ов or -ев declines like a Russian one.
- Guests are treated generously, and a той can have hundreds of guests.
