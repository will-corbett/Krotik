# Dialogues for review

Written for Krotik's Words section. Each needs a native speaker's check before it is published: is it natural, everyday Russian? Please mark changes by line number. When a dialogue is approved, its `reviewed` field in `content/words/<theme>.yaml` gets the reviewer's name and the date.

## Food and drink: Breakfast

`content/words/food.yaml`, dialogue 1

| # | Russian | English |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | **Мама:** Доброе утро! Ты будешь завтракать? | Good morning! Are you going to have breakfast? |
| 2 | **Сын:** Да. А что у нас на завтрак? | Yes. What's for breakfast? |
| 3 | **Мама:** Каша, хлеб с маслом и сыр. | Porridge, bread and butter, and cheese. |
| 4 | **Сын:** А яйца есть? | Are there any eggs? |
| 5 | **Мама:** Есть. Тебе одно или два? | There are. One or two for you? |
| 6 | **Сын:** Два, пожалуйста. И чай с молоком. | Two, please. And tea with milk. |
| 7 | **Мама:** Сахар положить? | Shall I put sugar in? |
| 8 | **Сын:** Нет, спасибо. Я пью без сахара. | No, thanks. I drink it without sugar. |
