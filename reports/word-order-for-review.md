# Word order practice for review

Each item: a question and two orders of the same answer. The first answer is the one we think sounds natural as a reply (the new information last). Please check both: is the first natural, and is the second really the less natural reply? Mark changes by number. When an item is approved, its `reviewed` field in `content/word-order.yaml` gets the reviewer's name and the date.
